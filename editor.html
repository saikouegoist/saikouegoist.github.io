how your data files are organized

Everything is separated into individual, easy-to-edit files inside the data/ folder:

  • data/articles.js: Long-form articles. Each article is a simple object. To remove an article, simply delete its block!
  • data/notes.js: Short thoughts, logs, and snippets. Filterable by year and keyword.
  • data/projects.js: Portfolio projects with tech tags, year, and repository links.
  • data/about.js: Biography, hobbies/interests, and hardware/software setup table.
  • data/links.js: Curated web directory organized by category.
  • data/config.js: Site name, tagline, stats, warning box text, and random thoughts.
